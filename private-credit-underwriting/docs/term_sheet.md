# Illustrative debt term sheet — not legally binding

**Borrower:** Atlas Industrial Services (fictional)

**Transaction:** Acquisition financing on a cash-free/debt-free basis

**Facilities:** €90m senior secured first-lien **unitranche**, one modeled class; no revolver or ancillary lines modeled

**Tenor:** Five years from closing

**Interest:** 7.5% annual cash coupon for Base; downside/severe stress +200/+300 basis points. Simplified BOP debt interest convention; there is no daily accrual or base-rate floor.

**Fees:** 2.0% lender upfront fee at closing

**Amortization:** 1.0% of original principal annually, and 50% modelled excess available cash above €5m minimum year-end cash balance

**Security:** Assumed senior-secured for modeling purposes only; legal perfection, location of assets, subsidiary guarantees and lien ranking need independent review

**Modeled financial covenants:** Max 6.0x net leverage; minimum 1.75x EBITDA / cash interest; minimum €5m cash. Tested annually in model. No legal interpretation of cure provisions or default events is implied.

**Conditions precedent:** Quality-of-earnings review, third-party financial diligence, security and guarantor documentation, KYC/AML, cash reconciliation, investment committee approval, validated sponsor equity funding

**Information rights to negotiate:** Quarterly financial reporting, annual audited statements, covenant certificate, budget and rolling liquidity forecast

**Other critical terms to negotiate:** Debt incurrence/negative pledge, dividends and restricted payments, acquisition baskets, EBITDA add-backs, permitted disposals, portability, MFN protection, change of control, transfer rights, equity cure provisions, early repayment call protection, intercreditor agreement if relevant

**Disclaimer:** The terms above are teaching assumptions only and do not purport to replicate an actual executed private credit agreement.
